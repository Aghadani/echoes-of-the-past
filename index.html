A NOTE FROM THE STUDIO

History, with a
face of its own.

Echoes of the Past is an experimental mirror that reimagines your portrait through visual worlds inspired by history and speculative futures. Face landmarks guide each effect as you move.

Camera frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded. This prototype uses a face-landmark model downloaded from Google’s MediaPipe CDN when you start the camera.
